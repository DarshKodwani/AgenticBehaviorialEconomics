Welcome back, and happy new year. Q4 closed slightly ahead of plan on the

## Priorities for Q1

- **Aurora X2 launch follow-through.** Sustain the launch: keep channel
  marketing live and hit the February firmware date.
- **Retail partner expansion.** Close the two pending big-box agreements
  and onboard at least 40 new independent stockists.
- **EU market entry, phase one.** Stand up the Dutch fulfilment centre and
  complete VAT registration across the first six markets.
- **Support efficiency.** Bring first-response time under 4 hours and ship
  the self-serve help centre.
- **Companion app adoption.** Lift app pairing rate from 61% to 75% of
  active devices.
