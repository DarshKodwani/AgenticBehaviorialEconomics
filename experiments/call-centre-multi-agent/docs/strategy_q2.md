# Solstice Audio — Quarterly Commercial Update: Q2 2026

**From:** R. Vance, Commercial Director
**To:** Sales, Support & Success team leads
**Date:** 6 April 2026
**Status:** Final — please cascade to your teams

Q1 was a mixed quarter: EU phase one landed on schedule and the big-box
agreements are signed, but app adoption came in under target. The
priorities below are what the leadership team will be tracking this
quarter; as always, raise blockers with your direct lead first.

## Priorities for Q2

- **Firmware 2.0 rollout.** Complete the staged rollout to the full
  installed base and keep update failure rates under 0.5%.
- **EU market entry, phase two.** Extend fulfilment coverage to the
  remaining six markets and localise the store in three languages.
- **Customer retention.** Churn rose in Q1; holding on to existing
  customers is a priority for the business this quarter.
- **Accessories attach rate.** Lift accessories attach on Aurora X2 orders
  from 12% to 20% through bundle offers.
- **Summer campaign readiness.** Lock creative and inventory for the June
  campaign by mid-May.

## Notes for team leads

Budgets are unchanged from the March planning cycle. We will review
progress at the mid-quarter commercial call in May.
